# 📊 AI Research Papers Analysis

This project analyzes over 600 recent AI research papers using data collected from arXiv.org.  
The goal is to explore emerging trends, key themes, sentiments, and top contributors in the AI research landscape.

## 📁 Dataset
The dataset includes:
- Title
- Authors
- Publication Date
- Summary
- Link to the paper

## 📈 What This Notebook Covers
- Year-wise publication trends
- Most frequent keywords and research topics
- Sentiment analysis of abstracts
- Topic modeling using LDA
- Predictive analysis of AI research growth
- Top authors by publication count

## 🧠 Key Insights
- 2024 saw the highest number of AI papers published so far and number of papers have reached half way in 2025 so far.
- Most papers expressed a positive sentiment toward AI.
- Key themes include: AI Ethics, Trust, Human-AI Interaction, Governance, Generative AI, and Education.
- Liming Zu and Qinghua Lu were the most active researchers.
